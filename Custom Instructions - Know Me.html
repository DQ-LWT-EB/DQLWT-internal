AI Context Prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts for building your Claude and Copilot custom instructions, know me, how I work, my team, and what to avoid.

Internal working tool · v1.0 · Owner: Eilidh Blanchard (AI Lead) · August 2026

Why bother? Custom instructions are a one-time setup, tell your AI who you are once, and it remembers every chat after. No more explaining your role, tone or preferences from scratch each time. It takes about ten minutes, and every response comes back closer to a solid first draft starting point that you can use.

Do it in one go. Work through all four prompts back to back rather than spreading them out, it shouldn't take more than 20 minutes in total. Tip: use voice mode in Claude or Copilot to talk through your answers instead of typing them, it's faster and tends to sound more like you.

How to use this page

Each prompt below is a conversation starter, not a form. Copy it into a new chat in Claude or Copilot, answer the questions honestly, add attachments for context if you want, and let the AI write the draft. Paste that draft into the box underneath, this page holds onto it while your tab's open, so it can build your merge prompt in the last step.

How long should your instructions be?

Shorter drafts perform more reliably than long ones. Every sentence should change how the AI responds to you, if a line doesn't do that, cut it.

Need ideas? Borrow from here

Wikipedia keeps a running, well-researched list of AI writing tells. Worth a skim before you answer, here are a few examples to get you thinking:

  • Cursed vocabulary: delve, intricate, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, landscape, foster, testament, enhance, crucial
  • Negative parallelisms: "It's not a product launch. It's a paradigm shift."
  • Rule-of-three lists: "innovative, transformative, and groundbreaking"
  • False ranges: "from intimate gatherings to global movements"
  • Excessive bolding and emoji section headers where plain prose would do
  • Em dashes doing the work a comma or full stop should
  • Reflexive summaries: "Overall," "In conclusion," even when nothing needs recapping

Full list: Wikipedia, Signs of AI writing
