DQ LWT Organisational Dashboard

This dashboard brings together data from Simpleview CRM across the Member Partner, Travel Trade, Bureau, Community Stakeholder, and Event user groups, alongside Act-On reporting, to provide the DQ/LWT team with a single view of performance and recent activity.

It is intended to support internal reporting, performance monitoring, operational management, and review of Simpleview-related activity across the organisation.
